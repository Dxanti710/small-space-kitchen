Leftovers that disappear to the back

Opaque takeout and sagging produce bags hide food until it is trash. Clear stackable fridge bins are a window. Some have a drain tray so greens are not sitting in a puddle.

The claim to check: you can pull the bin out like a drawer without unloading the shelf. If it only “looks organized” in a still photo and does not slide, skip it.

This is not a health claim. It is line-of-sight in a fridge.
