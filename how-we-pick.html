This is a conversion test, not a luxury haul. Amazon pays a small percent. Cheap, obvious-fix objects are the point.

  2. The job is visible in a still photo of a drawer. No app pairing.
  3. Claims are mechanical: slides, clips, stacks, spins. No “guaranteed tidy life.”
