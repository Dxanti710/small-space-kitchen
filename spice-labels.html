Spice jars you cannot read mid-cook

A round jar on its side hides the label. You open the drawer with a hot pan and guess. An angled in-drawer insert is a ramp so names face you.

Height is the trap. Many inserts want ~3 inches. A shallow junk drawer will not take one. If the listing does not show drawer height, skip it.

Jars are usually not included. Do not buy a second spice set just to fill a photo.
