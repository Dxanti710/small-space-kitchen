Why this site has no unboxing videos

We do not film kitchens and we do not show a face. Amazon’s original-content rule is commentary, analysis, or transformation — not a camera.

Each page here is written about one small-kitchen failure and one object type. Stills for Pinterest are graphics we made, not ripped Amazon listing photos. If we have not bought the item, we do not write “I use this.”

Paid Amazon links, when they exist, live on this hub. You click. We do not auto-redirect. We do not run paid ads to Amazon.
