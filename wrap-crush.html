Foil and parchment boxes crush too

Foil, plastic wrap, and parchment come in long cardboard. The drawer treats them like the bag boxes: stack, crush, unroll by accident.

An in-drawer wrap dispenser is a multi-slot stand so those boxes sit on edge. Same logic as a bag organizer, longer footprint. Measure drawer depth before you link one.

Cutter edges on some models are sharp. If you do not want that, pick a holder without a blade and keep the box’s own cutter.
